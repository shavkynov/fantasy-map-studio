# Constructive separable pair — isolated analytic geometry

Run node tools/world-separable-pair.cjs; echo "exit_code=$?". Optional --unit-only. One JSON and one compact four-panel HTML overview under artifacts, exclusive timestamp creation. No screenshot series needed.

This is an explicitly new analytic toy model, NOT a replacement source mask, fixture, baseline, natural coastline, K4 generator or app integration. It uses world-domain rotations/cell areas and existing assess. It does NOT pass through world-drift.fromSource or frozen-mask loading; frozenFixtureIntegrationRun=false is intentional. Existing blocked K4 remains unchanged as a control.

On a unit sphere choose a cap x>=cos(alpha), alpha=0.65 rad, and cut it by y=0. Piece0 owns y<0; piece1 owns y>=0. Half-open ownership disambiguates the zero-area touching seam at t=0 without cutting away land. Each exact piece area is pi*(1-cos(alpha)). Both pieces remain rigid; no deformation or changing area.

Opening poses are rotations about z by -rate*t and +rate*t, rate=0.25, t in[0,1]. Constructor requires 0<alpha<pi/2, positive rate/time and alpha+rate*maxTime<pi. Initial cap has longitude in[-alpha,alpha]; pieces have supports[-alpha,0] and[0,alpha]. At time t their support intervals are[-alpha-theta,-theta] and[theta,alpha+theta], theta=rate*t. No longitude seam crossing occurs under the precondition. For every real t>0 in the declared range these intervals are disjoint. This analytic argument is restricted to this model; it is not a general continuous-collision algorithm or acceptance of production geometry.

Rasters are separately evaluated at360x180 and720x360 for t=0,0.5,1, meridian0: six opening snapshots must pass assess. Source membership is evaluated analytically after inverse rotation, NOT via a newly rasterized canonical mask. Individual raster areas must approximate exact piece area within the unchanged3% tolerance. Negative control reverses motion at t=0.25 on360x180 and must be rejected for overlap. Always-empty or always-valid output cannot pass both area and negative-control assertions. Unit checks cover ownership, opening central gap, reversed-motion overlap, point round trips, interval separation, unchanged parameters and invalid bounds/time/id/grid.
