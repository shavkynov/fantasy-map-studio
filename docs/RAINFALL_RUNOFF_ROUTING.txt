RainfallRunoffRouting.build(field, drainage, {precipitation,runoffCoefficient}, initialDepth). Requires fixed acyclic receivers from SphericalDrainage for the SAME field; checks dimensions, land samples, receiver adjacency/cycles. Array/scalar P>=0 and C in [0,1], validated on land. Ocean samples ignored, ocean receivers must be -2. Exactly these two forcing keys; caller extracts P/C rather than passing E/seepage/externalInflow. Requires PRE-STEP water depths. Wet means depth>0, matching surface source generation, NOT inspector threshold 1e-9.

Dry land generates P*C*area as rates. Wet land receives P*area directly and generates no dry runoff. Generated dry runoff follows existing receivers, accumulating through dry cells and stopping at the first initially wet cell, existing water mask, or dry closed root. It is NOT routed onwards from a wet patch. Spill/release of old stored water, travel time, channel capacity, erosion, route losses, climate, calibrated discharge and persistent river/lake formation are absent. This diagnostic does not advance or replace the surface solver and does not modify inputs.

Returns localRunoff, flow and delivery arrays (NaN on ocean), kind codes and summary. flow is own+upstream dry-generated runoff through dry land; zero on initially wet land. delivery records only dry runoff at a wet target or the last dry ocean-outlet/closed-root cell. Codes: 0 dry nonterminal/ocean, 1 initially wet land, 2 dry water-outlet source, 3 dry closed terminal. Direct wet rain stays separate; do not add it twice or route it through the dry network. This is not the old accumulation array, which has steradian area units.
All returned numeric totals are rates: relative-height*steradian/model-time, NOT m3/s. Multiply by dt once for step inventories. rainLand=directWetRain+nonRunoff+dryRunoff; dryRunoff=toWet+toOcean+toClosed, with reported residuals. effectiveSurfaceInput=directWetRain+dryRunoff matches only the generated P/C source term, not stored water, final spill/export or total ledger with external basin inflow. Non-runoff remains an unmodelled sink, not soil water.
