BASIN FILL/SPILL RESOLUTION ON REAL A/B
Base commit: 16adf7e2c35efac3abe6407ececc8931091a63f0.
API: BasinWaterResolution.resolve(field, hierarchy, localExperimentSnapshot, BasinFillSpill).

The adapter adds in-basin storage and pending inventory once, validates the source ledger, invokes redistribution and returns a NEW merged-pool state plus a cumulative ledger with pendingTotal=0. Previous boundary exports remain accounted for but are not reintroduced as water. Old snapshots are not mutated; callers must replace the live inventory and must not replay the same pending source. This pure function does not enforce global once-only consumption across arbitrary callers.

IMPORTANT: output is {redistribution,ledger,metadata}, deliberately not an old local-budget snapshot. Merged storage may exceed first-local-spill capacities and must not go directly into BasinWaterBudget.advance. No coupled precipitation/evaporation timestep over merged pools is implemented here.

Executed Node tests on actual A/B 720x360 terrain. Exact blob-verified full A/B core, regional terrain, drainage, spill hierarchy, local water budget and fill/spill modules. SourceUV and spherical build extracted verbatim; experiment runner copied from approved source. No history, renderer, browser/Electron or climate run.

Prescribed initial experiment: P=.02,E=.001,C=.5, 100 steps dt=.05, from dry basins. These are synthetic forcing values in relative-height/model-time units, not planet climate. Redistribution is one instantaneous snapshot operation after that local-only experiment, not a simulation of rainfall and routing concurrently.
