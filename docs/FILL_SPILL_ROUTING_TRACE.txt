FILL/SPILL ROUTING TRACE — optional event journal, not UI activation
Baseline: 2b58b3835f2595ec2633f73490421d3ead7aa4a9. User gave general acceptance of .14 on Mac; not separate native checklist proof.

API
Fill.redistribute(field,hierarchy,volumes,{trackRouting:true}) adds routingTrace. Default or explicit false preserves the former output shape and allocates no journal arrays. Fourth-argument keys other than trackRouting and nonboolean values are rejected. Original arithmetic, tie order, routing decisions and 200000-operation bound are unchanged.
Journal: waterNode=B, copied initialByBasin, ordered events, actual stats {transfers,mergeCount,operations}, finalPools {node,volume}, boundaryOutflow. Initial pool nodes are 0..B-1; new merge nodes are B+1+merge index, distinct from external water node B. IDs are LOCAL TO ONE redistribute call, not stable world or cross-step IDs.
transfer {from,to,edge,volume} records positive moved inventory before mutation. boundary records positive exported inventory to waterNode. edge indexes the SAME immutable hierarchy.edges array. merge {node,left,right,edge} retires its child nodes and combines their inventories; it is NOT a flow or a counted transferred volume. Member sets can be reconstructed from merge events without copying all basin lists at every transfer. At most one journal event is recorded per processed operation.
Volumes are relative-height*steradian inventories, not rates. A parcel can cross multiple edges; summing transfer volumes gives gross algorithmic movement, NOT new water, net export or physical discharge. Do not divide events by dt and call them measured rivers. Old stored water and new rain are mixed; this journal does not attribute individual parcels to those sources.

SURFACE
Surface.create(...,{trackRouting:true}) records both actual redistribution calls in result.routingTrace.inflow and .recession. The latter is also result.redistribution.routingTrace by reference. No extra redistribute or advance is introduced. empty() remains untraced because no step occurred. Options can combine trackRouting and trackEvaporation. Missing Fill journal support throws when opted in, not a silent invented journal.
Inflow inventory is old stored water + accepted step input. Recession starts after actual evaporation/seepage and saddle splits; those losses/splits are not transfer events in the Fill journal. Export is the sum of the two boundaryOutflow values. The journal does not replace the surface ledger or existing splitEvents.
