SPHERICAL MOISTURE TRANSPORT — numerical moisture, NOT climate
Base HEAD: 05b1c6e2d4615d7783b0659e1411223e81692e4a.
User reported TEST .8 / water-ui2 working on Mac at 2026-11-04 10:46 MSK; individual checks not separately recorded. This package does not change that app or version.
New API: SphericalMoistureTransport.create({w,h,land}) -> empty(initialColumn=0), advance(state,forcing,dt), area copy, metadata. Even w 4..720, h 2..360. Model copies land and computes latitude/longitude cell areas in steradians, sum 4*pi; no planetary radius/age parameter changed.
state has amount (relative-column-depth*steradian inventory) and cumulative ledger. Return of advance is next state and includes stepStats, precipitationAmount (inventory per cell over the whole step), precipitation (step-average column depth/time). States bound to the creating context; no persistence/restore API. Recreate after geometry changes. Inputs not mutated; output arrays can be changed by caller, but invalid/imbalanced state is rejected.
Required forcing: east scalar or w*h array, south scalar or w*(h-1) array of signed tangent angular face velocities in radians/arbitrary time; oceanEvaporation and rainRate scalars or w*h nonnegative finite arrays. East face i connects cell i to right neighbor with longitude wrapping; south face y*w+x connects row y to y+1, positive south. East flux = east*dlat, south flux = south*cos(face latitude)*dlon in steradians/time. Polar exterior faces have zero area and no flux. These winds are prescribed, not a circulation solution.
Source on ocean cells only: oceanEvaporation*cell area. Infinite external reservoir; no ocean depletion, energy budget or land/lake evaporation feedback. Rain removes a prescribed fraction 1-exp(-rainRate*subdt) from post-advection moisture; rainRate is an inverse-time removal coefficient, NOT temperature/saturation, clouds or orographic precipitation. Rain over both land and ocean is separately accounted. No land routing, runoff or groundwater coupling yet.
Method: first-order donor-cell finite volume. Each substep: external ocean source, simultaneous upwind advection, exponential rain removal. Outgoing geometric flux / cell area sets CFL; internal substeps enforce CFL <= .8, capped at 1024; excessive demand rejected rather than silently clipping moisture or increasing the bound. Positive transport is not proof of physical completeness; method is numerically diffusive and timestep-dependent. Different CFL substepping also changes source/rain splitting. No stable-climate or convergence claim.
Budget: initialTotal + oceanSourceTotal = storedTotal + rainLandTotal + rainOceanTotal + residual. This is atmospheric bookkeeping with an external source, NOT a closed whole-planet water cycle. Precipitation output is averaged over dt; handing it to surface water is a later explicit coupling step, not temporal equivalence of both solvers. Do not add the prescribed spatial P separately on top of atmospheric rain.
Tests: node tools/spherical-moisture-transport-test.cjs
Passed synthetic 16x8 spherical tests: area 4*pi; zero forcing; analytic no-wind rain; constant zonal tracer; longitude seam both directions; north/south transport across unequal-area cells; ocean-only source; 60-step ocean-to-land-rain experiment; 40 seeded variable-wind/rate steps; positivity, immutability, invalid inputs, foreign context, bad ledger and CFL guard. Maximum ledger residual 4.440892098500626e-15.
60-step example: source 2.2619467105846467; atmosphere stored .5929993478479527; rain over land .41095810047920744; rain over ocean 1.2579892622574909. Relative units, not Earth-calibrated rates. No real A/B terrain, renderer, Electron or surface-core run in this package. No installer required.
