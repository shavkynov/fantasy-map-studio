API SurfaceWaterObjects.build(field, redistribution, {depthThreshold=1e-9}). Pass completed state.redistribution, not an atmospheric state or hierarchy.potentialDepth. Reads w,h,land,height,area and redistribution.w/h/depth. Ocean depth/height/area samples are ignored; production fill/spill uses ocean depth NaN. Land samples must be finite and valid. Inputs are not modified.

Objects are edge-connected land cells with depth strictly greater than threshold. Longitude wraps. Diagonal contacts and contacts through a pole point do not join objects; north/south map edges do not wrap. This is a geographic raster-patch convention, NOT the eight-neighbor drainage partition or the fill/spill pool graph. A single assigned pool can have multiple disconnected visible patches; different levels can occur within one patch. No claim of a persistent lake or river.

Returns Int32 labels (-1 for dry/ocean), objects and summary. Per object: scan-order id/seedCell, cell count, spherical area, volume, area-weighted mean/max depth, min/max terrain+depth, and whether an edge touches the ocean mask. Ocean-mask contact does not establish a physical outlet or connection to a calibrated ocean. IDs are local to one snapshot and must not be used as persistent project/name IDs.
Summary separates mapped wet volume from depth at/below threshold (omittedVolume). landVolume = mappedVolume + omittedVolume + numerical residual. This is geometric inventory, not a new hydrological mass-balance proof. Units: relative height, steradian area and relative-height*steradian volume; not metres, km2, m3 or flow rate. No minimum-area filtering, physical-state changes or new numerical dynamics.
