SPATIAL WATER FORCING — prescribed fields, NOT climate
Source HEAD: 9ef39e5f0759588814e4847807c1cc14ad667404.
New API: SpatialWaterForcing.build(field,{base,regions?,precipitationMean?}).
field: w,h, binary land mask, land area in steradians. Base requires scalar precipitation, evaporation, runoffCoefficient; seepage defaults to 0. Region: {u:[u0,u1],v:[v0,v1],rates:{...partial overrides}}. Cell centers use u=(x+.5)/w, v=(y+.5)/h; v=0 north, v=1 south. Bounds half-open; u0>u1 wraps seam; u=[0,1] spans full width. Overlap on selected LAND cells is rejected; ocean overlap has no effect. This is a geographic raster prescription, not material-coordinate transport.
Result: {forcing,summary,metadata}; forcing has fresh Float64Arrays P/E/C/seepage, NaN on ocean, directly accepted by CoupledBasinWater.advance(state,forcing,dt). Caller may mutate result arrays. Inputs not modified. Rebuild for changed field geometry. No UI connection yet; installed TEST .7 unchanged.
Optional precipitationMean rescales only P by target/(sum(P*area)/sum(area)) over land. Zero target gives zero P. Positive target with zero raw rainfall rejected. All-ocean field rejected. Invalid/negative/nonfinite rates, C>1, invalid areas, bounds, mask, overlaps and overflow rejected. Evaporation mean is a prescribed rate over land, NOT actual evaporation volume.
Equal area-mean P does NOT guarantee equal water input: core uses C on initially dry cells and direct rain on initially wet cells. No P*area sum is added separately; no duplicate rain input. No rainfall is drawn from or returned to ocean atmosphere here. External inflow is not generated by this builder.
