ABProjectWater.create(CoupledWaterUI, dependencies) returns the water interface plus setProjectLocked(boolean), prepare(index,end,progress), stageProject(candidate).
Dependencies: models from the LOWER diagnostic renderer (before water wrapping), compose, getContext, surface, fill, doc, win, getVisible, getSurface, recipe (real ABProjectRecipe in integration), extras for CoupledWaterUI. Exactly two A/B panes are expected. Use returned models/compose for Viewer.attach, then adapter.attach(view).

prepare matches the recipe replay callback signature. It requires the water lock, builds through the lower model chain (registering owner/cache identities), calls getContext and records provenance without replacing live water records. Those lower frame/data functions must not mutate visible DOM. Returned scenes are shallow frozen, not deep-frozen numerical data.
stageProject validates through recipe.validate, checks scene owner/index/lock epoch, progress/steps/scenario/feedback and state/context shape. It retains the replay-created bound states and contexts rather than serializing or cloning them. It cannot independently prove a numerical state belongs to its context; trusted replay and numerical core guards remain responsible.

Caller must lock viewer AND adapter, separately lock display selectors and all other conflicting UI, and keep locks until commit or rollback finishes. Lock acquisition can fail; an integrating caller must unwind any partially acquired locks. Attached water callbacks, direct exported water actions and frame creation are guarded. Private sync cannot enable protected buttons while adapter is locked. This is not a lock on arbitrary external DOM/record writes. Existing numerical work must finish or be cancelled before acquisition.

stageProject returns synchronous commit/rollback, suitable for ABProjectSession.stage. Commit registers exact replay frames through the existing water wrapper using a scoped frame-transfer route (no geometry rebuild), adopts both records, computes images, obtains viewer.stageProject and commits it, then updates water status/balance. The base composer must limit visible side effects to the snapshotted hydro labels; arbitrary composer side effects are NOT covered. No await occurs inside publication. It is synchronous publish-with-rollback, not browser/OS atomic storage.
rollback restores old water state/context/args/rain references, viewer transaction when present, hydro/water labels and protected control states. Record tokens remain monotonic. Caller must invoke rollback on commit error before unlocking. Rollback backend/DOM failures can propagate; no unconditional recovery from a broken backend. A pre-commit discard changes nothing. Abandoned frames are excluded from water overlay by the public composer.
On success, unlock viewer then adapter; continue/dry use restored contexts. Future Apply builds through original lower models and resets water normally. Full UI still needs recipe/session wiring, reliable combined lock ownership, display-selector blocking, native dialogs/progress/cancel and acceptance.
