ATMOSPHERE -> SURFACE WATER — one-way coupling, NOT a closed planet cycle
Base HEAD: 58cd67f9e514e356224bc629928572c4843f49f8.
API: AtmosphereSurfaceWater.create(field,hierarchy,SphericalMoistureTransport,CoupledBasinWater,BasinFillSpill) -> empty(initialAtmosphericColumn=0), advance(state,{atmosphere,surface},dt), metadata. Surface starts empty. field needs w,h,land,height,area; hierarchy as in the existing surface core. Land, height, area, basin mapping, basin records and edge records are copied. Cell areas must match the atmospheric spherical grid to relative tolerance 1e-12, including ocean cells. Recreate after geometry changes.
Atmosphere forcing: east, south, oceanEvaporation, rainRate, as in SPHERICAL_MOISTURE_TRANSPORT.txt. Surface forcing: evaporation and runoffCoefficient required, seepage optional zero; scalar or cell arrays. Explicit surface precipitation, externalInflow, and unknown keys rejected, including at top level. No hidden default spatial rain. C must be in [0,1].
State: {atmosphere,water,nonRunoffTotal,ledger,stepStats?}, bound to context. Advance is pure relative to inputs; caller replaces live state. No persistence/restore or preloaded surface inventory API.
Step order: (1) atmospheric advance; (2) its step-average precipitation becomes the ONLY surface P; (3) existing surface advance at the same dt, using its initial wet mask. Rain on initially wet land goes directly to surface water, dry land gets rain*C. NonRunoff = rain*(1-C) on initially dry land, explicitly accumulated as an unmodelled external sink, NOT groundwater storage. It is computed from rain inventory and initial wet mask, not from a diagnostic cutoff. No dt-normalization or precipitation mean rescaling is applied.
Total identity: initialAtmosphere + initialSurface + oceanSource = atmosphereStored + surfaceStored + rainOcean + nonRunoff + surfaceBoundary + surfaceEvaporation + surfaceSeepage + residual. Rain over land is an INTERNAL transfer and is not added again to the total RHS. Surface evaporation/seepage are external sinks, not atmospheric return; rainOcean and surfaceBoundary are distinct ocean-directed sinks. Ocean source remains prescribed/infinite. No whole-planet reservoir closure, energy, temperature, pressure, circulation or climate claim.
Temporal limitation: atmospheric internal CFL substeps are averaged into one surface step. Surface wet mask/loss geometry follow its existing order. This is NOT synchronous microstep coupling, not a convergence proof, and not stable lakes.
